Why independent matters

A firm that sells damp treatment cannot give you an impartial opinion about damp treatment.

That conflict is the reason so many London homeowners end up paying for injected damp courses, hacked-off plaster and tanking they never needed. ATI has no treatment arm, no contractor partners and no referral fees. Our only product is the report.

Zero commercial interest

We do not carry out repairs, we do not subcontract them, and we take nothing from anyone who does. Findings are findings.

Measured, not guessed

Calibrated moisture and hygrometry readings, salt analysis where relevant, thermal imaging, and readings recorded room by room.

A report you can act on

Cause, extent, photographic evidence, a specification of what genuinely needs doing, and what does not. Written to be read by a third party.

Stands up to scrutiny

What we report on

One report type. Many things it can settle.

Whatever the symptom, the inspection is the same forensic process. Choose what you are dealing with to see how we approach it and what the report will tell you.

Suspected Rising Damp

Genuine rising damp is far rarer in London housing stock than the number of injected damp courses would suggest. The same tide mark can be produced by hygroscopic salts in old plaster, a bridged or raised external ground level, a defective gully, or a slow leak beneath a floor.

We profile moisture at depth as well as at the surface, test for salt contamination, check external levels and drainage, and only then state whether capillary rise is actually occurring. In a good number of London inspections, it is not.

Water Ingress

Lateral ingress in London terraces and mansion blocks usually traces back to something outside the affected room: perished pointing on an exposed elevation, a cracked parapet or valley, a blocked hopper, failed flashing at a rear addition, or a neighbour's overflow.

We inspect the external envelope alongside the internal damage and correlate the two, so the report identifies the defect rather than the stain. Where access limits what can be seen, the report says so explicitly instead of speculating.

Condensation & Humidity

London flats and converted houses generate a great deal of moisture in small, well-sealed volumes. Where extraction is inadequate and surfaces are cold, condensation follows, and it is frequently misdiagnosed as structural damp.

We record internal temperature, relative humidity, dew point and surface temperatures, assess existing extraction against the room use, and set out whether the cause is building fabric, ventilation provision, occupancy, or a combination. This distinction matters enormously in a dispute.

Mould Assessment

Mould is a symptom with several possible causes, and treating the surface without establishing which one you have is why it returns. Landlords and tenants often hold entirely opposite views about the reason, which is precisely why an independent view is useful.

The report records the extent and distribution of growth, the environmental conditions that permit it, the building defects contributing to it, and the remedial and ventilation measures required, in priority order.

Timber Decay & Woodworm

Timber decay follows moisture, so a timber inspection is a moisture inspection. We examine accessible floor timbers, joist ends built into external walls, sub-floor ventilation, cellars and roof timbers.

The report distinguishes active from historic infestation, wet rot from dry rot, and cosmetic from structural loss. Where structural adequacy is in question we say what further opening up or engineering input is needed, rather than guessing at it.

Basements & Cellars

London basement conversions and lower-ground flats are the single most common source of contested damp problems. Failed cementitious tanking, membrane systems installed over unresolved defects, and sumps that were never maintained are all routine findings.

We assess the existing waterproofing against BS 8102 principles, identify what type of system is present and whether it was ever appropriate, and report on what would be required to achieve the intended grade of use.

Pre-Purchase Reports

A valuation survey that recommends a specialist damp report is a starting point, not an answer. Buyers frequently receive a contractor's free quote in its place, which is not an independent assessment and carries no professional liability.

Our pre-purchase report gives you the cause, the extent, the remedial specification and an outline cost, in a form your solicitor can use and your seller can be shown. It is often the difference between a renegotiation and a guess.

Landlord & Disrepair

Where a tenant has reported damp or mould, an independent inspection establishes the facts before positions harden. Reports are prepared knowing they may be read by the tenant, an environmental health officer, an ombudsman or a court.

Findings are set out neutrally: what is a fabric defect and the landlord's responsibility, what relates to ventilation provision, and what relates to occupancy. Recommendations are prioritised by risk to health and to the building.

Second Opinions

If you have been quoted for injection, hacking off plaster, tanking or a full replastering programme and something about it does not sit right, a second opinion costs a fraction of the works.

We review the existing quotation or report, inspect independently, and tell you whether the recommended scope is justified, partially justified, or unsupported by the evidence. We will say so plainly either way.

Post-Works Verification

Remedial damp work is easy to pay for and hard to verify. Once plaster is back on and paint is dry, whether the specification was actually followed becomes very difficult to establish.

We inspect during or after works against the original specification and record what was done, what was omitted, and whether the moisture profile has actually improved. Useful for a retention release, an insurer, or a dispute.

Landlords, agents & solicitors

When damp becomes a dispute, the only useful opinion is a neutral one.

Awaab's Law and the tightening of hazard assessment have put fixed deadlines around reported damp and mould in rented London housing. Tenants are better informed, disrepair claims are being brought more readily, and a contractor's quote is not evidence.

ATI is instructed by private landlords, managing agents, housing providers and solicitors on both sides. Because we sell no remedial work, our findings carry weight with parties who would dismiss a contractor's assessment out of hand.

Straight answers

Fair questions about paying for a survey.

What does an independent survey cost?

We quote a fixed fee before we attend, based on the property type and the scope you need. You will know the figure in our same day reply and there is no obligation to proceed. We do not quote for remedial work, so the fee is the whole of our commercial interest in your property.

Why not just use the free survey a damp company offers?

Because it is a sales visit, not a survey. It carries no professional liability, it is produced by the party who will be paid for the treatment, and it will very rarely conclude that no work is required. Our report has no work attached to it.

Do you carry out the repairs afterwards?

No, and we never will. That independence is the product. The report specifies what needs doing in enough detail for you to obtain comparable quotes from contractors of your own choosing.

Can you recommend a contractor?

We deliberately do not, because a referral relationship would compromise the report. We will tell you what trade you need, what the specification must include, and what to be wary of in the quotes you receive.

How long does the inspection take?

Usually 60 to 90 minutes for a flat or standard terrace, longer for a larger house, a basement, or a full timber inspection. We will not rush it, and we will not open up fabric without your consent.

When do I get the report?

Within 24 hours of the inspection, by email, as a PDF. If anything found requires urgent attention we will tell you at the property rather than wait for the document.

Will the report help me renegotiate a purchase?

That is one of its most common uses. Because it states cause, extent and an outline cost of the remedial specification, it gives your solicitor something concrete to put to the seller instead of a vague concern.

Which parts of London do you cover?

All of it, north and south of the river, across every postcode district in London. Concentrating on one city's housing stock is a large part of why our diagnoses hold up, so put your postcode in the form and we will confirm the same day.

Do you work for landlords and tenants?

Both, and for solicitors and agents acting for either. We take no position beyond what the evidence supports, which is why parties on opposite sides are willing to accept the same report.
