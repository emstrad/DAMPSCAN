Roof repairs

Most repairs are small. The hard part is finding what is actually letting water in, rather than the place where it appears.

Water does not fall straight down

A stain on a bedroom ceiling is where water stopped, not where it started. It will have run along a rafter, across a ceiling joist, and down a nail before it showed. That is why a repair aimed at the stain fixes nothing and why the second roofer often finds what the first one missed.

We work from the loft side as well as the roof where there is access. A wet rafter tells you the run, a dry one narrows the field, and daylight through a slate answers the question outright.

What usually turns out to be the cause

In order of how often we find it: a mortar fillet where lead should be, at a chimney or an abutment. A valley that has rusted or lifted at the lap. Slipped or broken slates from nail fatigue. A cracked or displaced ridge tile. And, more often than anyone expects, a gutter that has been overflowing behind a fascia for two winters and rotting the timber ends.

The other common answer is not a leak at all. Condensation in a loft with blocked eaves ventilation soaks insulation and marks ceilings in exactly the same pattern, usually worst in the coldest weeks and nowhere near a roof defect.

What a proportionate repair looks like

Replace the failed component with the right material, not with sealant. Lead where lead belongs, a new valley rather than a patch over the hole, matching slates fixed properly rather than hooked on a clip and hoped over. Where we can see the same failure starting elsewhere on the roof, we tell you now rather than leaving it to be next year's callout.

We photograph the defect before we touch it and send those photographs with the quote, so you are buying a repair you can see.

When a repair is the wrong answer

If we are replacing slipped slates for the third time in different places, the nails have gone and every repair is a deposit on the same problem. At that point we will say so and price both options, so the decision is yours with the numbers in front of you rather than ours.

Questions

How quickly can you come out?

We reply the same day, and sooner if water is coming in. Where it is actively leaking we will make it safe first and price the permanent repair afterwards.

Do you charge to come and look?

No. Coming out and giving you a price costs you nothing.

Is a repair guaranteed?

Yes, in writing, on the work we have done. What we cannot guarantee is the rest of a roof we did not replace, and we will say plainly which is which.
