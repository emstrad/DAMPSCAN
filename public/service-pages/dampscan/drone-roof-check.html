A drone roof check, on the same visit as your damp survey

A large share of the damp we are asked to diagnose in Kent starts high up: a slipped tile, a cracked chimney flaunching, a lead flashing that has lifted, or a gutter quietly overflowing down a wall. Most of it cannot be seen properly from the ground, and nobody wants to pay for scaffolding to find out. The drone check puts a camera within a few feet of every part of the roof while we are already there for the survey, so the cause of an upstairs damp patch is photographed rather than guessed.

When it is worth adding

  • Damp on a bedroom ceiling, an upstairs wall or a chimney breast
  • You are buying and the roof cannot be seen from the street or the garden
  • Staining that gets worse after heavy rain and wind
  • A roofer has quoted for work and you want to see the problem for yourself
  • An older roof that has not been looked at closely for years

What the drone looks at

Every slope of the roof covering, ridge and hip tiles, valleys, chimney stacks and their flaunching and pots, lead flashings and soakers where the roof meets a wall, dormers and skylights, parapets, and the gutters and downpipes from above, where blockages and failed joints show up best. On a flat roof, the surface, the upstands and the outlets.

We take still photographs and short video passes, then pick out anything that bears on the damp inside, so the report links what is wrong on the roof to the reading on the wall beneath it.

How it fits with the damp survey

The drone check is an add-on, only booked with a damp survey, because on its own a picture of a roof is not a diagnosis. Together they answer the question that matters: where is the water getting in, and what should be done about it in what order. It adds about half an hour to the visit and appears as its own section in the same written report, delivered the day after.

We fly in line with the Civil Aviation Authority rules and only where the site and the weather allow it safely. If we cannot fly on the day, for wind, rain or a restriction we could not know about in advance, the check is not charged and the survey goes ahead as normal.

Independent, as with everything we do

DampScan does not repair roofs or sell any building work, so the photographs are there to show you what is happening, not to start a quote. Where work is needed, the report says what and where, so any roofer you choose is pricing the same thing.

Questions

Can I book the drone check on its own?

No. It is only offered with a damp survey, because the pictures are most useful when someone is also inside reading the walls and working out how the water travels. Booked together, the visit and the report cover the whole route the water takes.

What if the weather is bad on the day?

We only fly when it is safe and allowed. If wind or rain stops us, or there is a restriction at the property we could not have known about, the drone check is not charged and the rest of the survey goes ahead.

Do you need access to my neighbours' gardens?

No. The drone flies from your own property and is kept over it as far as possible. We tell you before we start and keep the flight short. Photographs are of your roof, not your neighbours.
