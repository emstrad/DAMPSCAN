Independent damp surveys: the diagnosis, with nothing to sell

A damp survey should tell you what is wrong, why, and what to do about it, in enough detail that you could take the report to anybody. Most of what gets called a damp survey is an inspection carried out by a firm that sells the remedy, which is a different thing with a different incentive. We carry out no treatment of any kind, so the report is the whole of what we sell.

When a survey is worth having

  • A homebuyer or building survey has flagged damp and recommended a specialist
  • You have been quoted for invasive damp proofing and want to know whether it is justified
  • Mould keeps returning in the same place each winter despite being cleaned
  • You are a landlord or agent facing a reported damp and mould issue
  • Work has been carried out already and the problem has come back

What the survey covers

Calibrated moisture readings taken across a grid rather than at the spots that look worst, so the shape and extent of the moisture is recorded and not just its peak. Surface temperatures, air temperature and relative humidity where condensation is in question, so the dew point conditions are on the record.

A full external inspection, which is where most of the answers are: ground levels against every elevation, drainage and gully condition, rainwater goods, roof coverings, flashings and junctions, pointing and render, and the presence and height of any existing damp proof course. High level elements are inspected properly rather than assessed from the ground.

Internally, the construction, the finishes, the ventilation provision and its measured performance, the condition of any exposed timber, and the underfloor void where it can be accessed.

What you get

A written report the day after the visit. Photographs, the readings and where they were taken, the diagnosed cause, and what should be done in order of priority. Where we have not been able to establish something without opening up, the report says so and says exactly where to open, rather than leaving it vague.

Where work is needed, it is written as a specification any competent contractor can price, so the quotes you collect are for the same job. Where it is not needed, the report says that just as clearly, and a survey concluding that no damp proofing is required is a routine outcome here.

Why the survey should come from someone with nothing to sell

Because the two most expensive mistakes in this trade both come from doing it the other way round. Treating a symptom that has a different cause means paying for work that cannot succeed, and the failure shows up two or three years later once the replastering has dried. And specifying a remedy before establishing the cause tends to produce the remedy the specifier sells.

Most repeat damp problems we are called to are second visits to work that treated a symptom. That is the whole reason the sequence matters.

Questions

What does a damp survey cost?

It depends on the size of the property and how much is in question, which is why the fee is confirmed when you enquire rather than quoted here as a headline. What is worth comparing it against is the cost of the treatment it might avoid: a survey is a small fraction of a typical injection and replastering quote for a ground floor, and a meaningful share of our surveys conclude that the treatment is not required. Our current fee bands are on the prices page.

Will you try to sell me the work afterwards?

No. DampScan does not carry out remedial work, so there is no quote to follow the report. It is written to stand on its own, with enough detail that you could hand it to any competent contractor and compare like for like prices. That separation is deliberate, because a diagnosis that only makes sense if you buy the remedy is not a diagnosis.

How long does the visit take and when do I get the report?

A typical survey takes between one and two hours on site depending on the size of the property and the extent of the problem, longer where a full timber inspection or underfloor access is involved. The written report follows the day after the visit. We respond to every enquiry the same day and arrange the visit around you, including evenings and weekends where that is what works.
