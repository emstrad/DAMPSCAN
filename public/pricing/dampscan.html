What a survey costs, and what decides it

Plenty of Kent damp firms will survey for nothing, and then quote for the treatment. We do it the other way: you pay a published fee for the survey and the written report, and we carry out no remedial work, so there is nothing to sell you afterwards. The report tells you what does not need doing as plainly as what does.

The price you see is the price you pay. The survey fee is agreed in writing before we attend and does not change afterwards, whatever we find. Extras such as a timber assessment or an invasive check are priced below, and nothing is added unless you agree to it first.

Which size is my property?

Localised damp survey: £215

A specific problem in a specific place: one damp wall, one stained ceiling, one room with mould. The rest of the property is not inspected, so if the cause turns out to sit elsewhere we will say so and tell you what a wider look would cost. We do not simply widen the survey and bill you for it.

Full house survey: £295

Most semis and terraces. A whole property inspected inside and out, whether or not you have spotted a problem in every room. This is the right choice before a purchase, for a landlord facing a complaint, or when damp has appeared in more than one place and you do not yet know whether they are related.

Large property survey: From £375

Larger houses, which take longer to inspect properly rather than being harder. The figure moves with the size of the building and how much of it is accessible. We confirm the exact fee in writing before we attend, and it does not move afterwards.

Very large or period property: From £450

Buildings where the construction itself is the complication: multiple build eras, cellars, outbuildings, extensions meeting original fabric. Quoted individually after we know what the building is. Ask and we will give you a figure, not a range, before you commit to anything.

What the extra checks are

Localised timber assessment

Accessible timbers checked for decay, fungal growth and woodworm, with a timber report.

Full-house timber assessment

The whole accessible home checked for timber decay and woodworm, with a timber report.

Targeted invasive investigation

Where normal access is not enough: a small opening, agreed with you before we start.

Underfloor camera check

A camera under suspended timber floors to see the joists and ventilation without lifting boards.

Low-level cavity bridging check

Checks for rubble or mortar in the bottom of a cavity wall bridging damp across.

Invasive checks, if needed: from £85 on the same visit. If something can only be settled by opening up, we carry the tools for it: a small incision in plaster, a length of skirting off, a floorboard lifted. Agreed with you before we start, never after.

What every survey includes
  • A qualified surveyor on site for as long as the property takes, not a fixed appointment slot
  • Calibrated moisture readings taken to depth, not surface readings from a pin meter
  • Surface temperature and humidity readings where condensation has to be excluded rather than assumed
  • External inspection: ground levels, drainage, gutters, pointing, roof junctions and anything else feeding water into the building
  • A written report within 24 hours of the visit, with photographs and the readings behind every conclusion
  • A clear statement of what does not need doing, and where a quote you are holding goes beyond the defect
  • A specification detailed enough for contractors to price like for like. We never bid for the work ourselves
What is never added to the bill

Nothing is added for travel

Anywhere across our Kent and South East coverage, the agreed fee is the fee. Fuel, tolls and parking are our problem, not a line on your invoice. Properties inside London are surveyed by our sister practice, ATi Damp Survey, and priced the same way.

Evenings and weekends cost no more

We work around tenants and working days as a matter of course. There is no out of hours premium.

A second visit, if you need one

If you need us back after works to verify what was done, that is a shorter visit and priced accordingly. It is never assumed or added automatically.

Questions about the price

Other Kent firms survey for free. Why pay you?

Because a free survey is usually paid for by the treatment it recommends. We carry out no remedial work at all, so finding something earns us nothing, and finding nothing costs us nothing. Every conclusion in the report is shown with the readings and photographs it rests on, it says plainly what does not need doing, and where a quote you already hold goes further than the defect it says that too.

I have damp in one room of a four bedroom house. Which band?

Start with the localised survey at £215. If the cause is where the damp is, that is the whole job and you have paid the smallest fee that answers the question. If the readings point somewhere else in the building, we will tell you on site what a full survey would cost and you can decide there and then. We would rather sell you the £215 twice than the £375 once for no reason.

What if you find nothing wrong?

The fee is the same, and the report is arguably worth more. Establishing that a property does not have a damp problem is a real finding, and it is the one that saves people the most money, whether that is a purchase that proceeds or a quote that gets refused. You are paying for the inspection and the written conclusion, not for a list of defects.

Can the price change after you arrive?

The survey fee cannot, including if the property turns out to be larger or more awkward than described. The only way your total ends up higher than the band price is if you agree on the day to an invasive inspection, which starts at £85 for the visit. We will only suggest it where opening up would actually change the conclusion, and if it would not we will tell you that instead.
