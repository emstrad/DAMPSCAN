The questions people ask before spending money on cooling.

How much does air conditioning cost to install?

A single wall-mounted split in a straightforward room is the entry point, with multi-split and ducted systems rising from there depending on the number of rooms, the equipment and how much making good is involved. We do not price a job we have not seen, because a price given blind is a guess that gets corrected upwards later. The visit is free and the written quote is fixed.

Do I need planning permission?

For most domestic installs, no. Outdoor units on houses usually fall under permitted development provided siting, size and noise conditions are met, but flats, listed buildings, conservation areas and front elevations are different. We check this when we visit and tell you before you commit.

Does it heat as well as cool?

Every system we fit does both. Run in reverse it is an air to air heat pump, and in a room you currently heat with electricity it is the cheapest heat you can buy, because it moves heat rather than making it.

How noisy is it?

Indoors, quieter than a fridge. The outdoor unit is the one worth thinking about because it sits near somebody, so we site it for the neighbour as well as for you and tell you its sound level before it goes in.

Will it fix condensation or damp?

No, and this is the most common reason we are asked to quote for the wrong thing. Condensation is a moisture and ventilation problem, and cooling a surface can make it worse. The answer is extract, positive input or heat recovery, and it usually costs less.

How often does it need servicing?

Annually for a domestic split in normal use, and more often where it runs year round or serves a comms room. Most manufacturers also require documented servicing to keep the warranty, so it is rarely optional in practice.

Can you just regas my old system?

We can, and on its own it is money thrown away. Refrigerant sits in a sealed circuit and is not used up, so a system that is low has a leak. We find the leak first, and venting refrigerant deliberately is an offence besides.

What happens to my details?

They go to the person pricing your job and nowhere else. Not sold, not shared with lead brokers, not added to a marketing list.
