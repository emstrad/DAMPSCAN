Damp proofing in Chelsea: an independent survey first

Damp proofing in Chelsea is rarely about a missing damp proof course. It is about water getting into front vaults and lower ground floors, basements whose drainage has stopped working, and stucco fronted walls that take in rain. Those need the source found and fixed, not a wall injected. ATi surveys only and sells no treatment, so the report is about the cause.

Why the survey comes before damp proofing in Chelsea

A great deal of the damp proofing sold in Chelsea is sold to a wall that is wet for another reason: a lightwell or patio above the old course, a defective gutter on a party parapet, a bathroom extract ducted into a void, or condensation in a flat that has lost its air changes. We do not sell damp proofing or any other remedial work, so the survey has one job, which is to say which of those it is.

The two quotes we see most in Chelsea are re-tanking a basement that has a sound membrane but a silted channel or a failed pump, and injecting the lower walls of a stucco terrace whose real problem is a leaking vault, a blocked lightwell drain or cracked render above. Both are expensive and both leave the cause where it was.

In Chelsea, a large share of homes are flats, and damp proofing quotes are often given to a leaseholder for a problem that belongs to the building. Before paying, check whether the water is coming through the roof, the external walls, the front area or a shared drain, because under most leases those are the freeholder's to repair. Our report states the source and the route, with the evidence a managing agent needs.

Damp proofing methods, briefly

A chemical damp proof course, a physical course, a waterproof render, a drained membrane, a breathable plaster: each is sold as damp proofing, and each answers a different question. The survey's job is to establish which question the Chelsea wall is actually asking.

In converted buildings the answer is often outside the flat altogether: a communal gutter, a front area drain, a parapet or a neighbour's raised garden. A damp proofing system inside the flat cannot fix any of those.

Chelsea walls, and what they actually need

Chelsea's terraces are solid brick behind stucco, built in lime. They stay dry when the stucco is sound and painted in a breathable finish, the parapets and valley gutters are clear, and the vaults and lightwells drain freely. Modern basements stay dry while their channels are clear and their pumps are serviced.

  • Servicing basement drainage: clearing channels and testing pumps, alarms and battery backup
  • Repairing pavement vaults, coal hole covers and the asphalt over them
  • Repairing cracked stucco and repainting in a breathable system
  • Clearing and repairing lightwell drains, parapets and valley gutters

Vaults, lightwells and the street above

Many Chelsea houses have vaults running out under the pavement, once used for coal, with a lightwell in front of the lower ground floor. The vault roof is the pavement, and its waterproofing is the asphalt and the coal hole cover above it. When either fails, water drips into the vault and soaks into the front wall of the house below ground level.

Fixing that is a repair to the pavement waterproofing and the drainage of the lightwell, sometimes with the highway authority or the freeholder involved, rather than a treatment to the wall. A survey that shows where the water enters, with readings and photographs, is what makes that conversation straightforward.

What proper damp proofing involves in a London building

  • A diagnosis in writing, with readings, before any specification
  • External and communal faults identified and assigned: parapets, gutters, lightwells, shared drains
  • A new damp proof course only on a Chelsea wall that genuinely lacks a working one at low level
  • Breathable or salt resistant replastering chosen for the wall, not a default system
  • Consent considered where the building is listed or in a conservation area
  • A record of what was done and why, which a future buyer's surveyor will ask for

Warning signs in a damp proofing quote

  • Rising damp diagnosed on an upper floor, or on a party wall with no ground contact
  • No inspection of the lightwell, front area, rear yard or parapet
  • A blanket timber treatment added to a damp quote
  • A guarantee that does not say which walls, or what happens if the cause was elsewhere
  • A price for a Chelsea terrace with no reference to its solid walls or its lime plaster
  • Pressure to agree before a buyer's or lender's deadline

When a damp proof course is the right answer

We are not opposed to damp proofing, only to it being sold as the answer before the question has been asked. Where the readings, the pattern and the building all point to a missing or failed course in a Chelsea property, the report recommends one and says how it should be done.

The survey fee is published and fixed before the visit. In Chelsea, where replastering and making good in a period flat is expensive and disruptive, establishing whether the work is needed before it starts is the economical step.

The report gives you the cause, the responsibility and a specification. In Chelsea that often means a letter to a freeholder or managing agent with the evidence attached, before any work inside the flat is considered.

Damp proofing questions in Chelsea

Our Chelsea basement is leaking. Does it need re-tanking?

Usually not. Drained membrane systems fail at their serviceable parts first: channels silt up, pumps fail and alarms are switched off. The survey checks the system and says what needs servicing or repairing.

The front wall of our lower ground floor is damp. Is it rising damp?

Far more often it is the vault under the pavement, a leaking coal hole cover or a blocked lightwell drain. The survey traces the water to its source.

Who pays to fix damp in a Chelsea mansion flat?

It depends on the lease and the source. Roofs, external walls and structure are commonly the freeholder's. Our report identifies the source clearly so the right party can be asked to act.

Can repairs work within the conservation area rules?

Yes. The report recommends repairs that suit the building: drainage, rainwater, breathable render and lime, rather than treatments that change its character.

Can you check a damp proofing or tanking quote?

Yes. Send it with your request and the report will say which parts are supported by the evidence and which are not.

Do you recommend contractors?

No. We take no referral fees. The report gives a clear specification any competent contractor can price, so you can compare like with like.

Can you recommend a damp proofing company in Chelsea?

We do not refer work or take commission, which keeps the report independent. What we give you instead is a specification precise enough that any competent contractor can price it, and we are happy to read the quotes you receive against it.
