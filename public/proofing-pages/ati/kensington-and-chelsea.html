Damp proofing in Kensington and Chelsea: an independent survey first

In Kensington and Chelsea, "damp proofing" usually means one of two things: a basement that is letting water in, or a stucco fronted house or mansion flat with damp on a wall. Both involve buildings where the wrong treatment is expensive to undo and the right answer often sits with a freeholder, a neighbour or the original basement contractor. ATi carries out no remedial work, so the survey is only about what is actually wrong.

Why the survey comes before damp proofing in Kensington and Chelsea

A great deal of the damp proofing sold in Kensington and Chelsea is sold to a wall that is wet for another reason: a lightwell or patio above the old course, a defective gutter on a party parapet, a bathroom extract ducted into a void, or condensation in a flat that has lost its air changes. We do not sell damp proofing or any other remedial work, so the survey has one job, which is to say which of those it is.

The quotes we are most often asked to review here are re tanking a basement that has a working drained membrane with a failed pump or silted channel, and injecting the ground floor walls of a stucco terrace that are wet from cracked render, a blocked front area drain or a leaking pavement vault. Neither addresses the cause, and both can run to very large sums in this borough.

In Kensington and Chelsea, a large share of homes are flats, and damp proofing quotes are often given to a leaseholder for a problem that belongs to the building. Before paying, check whether the water is coming through the roof, the external walls, the front area or a shared drain, because under most leases those are the freeholder's to repair. Our report states the source and the route, with the evidence a managing agent needs.

What the different damp proofing treatments are for

Injection is meant for rising damp in a wall with no working course. Tanking and cavity drain membranes are for below ground walls, which in Kensington and Chelsea usually means a lower ground floor or a cellar. Salt resistant replastering deals with contamination left by long term damp. Ventilation and heating deal with condensation. Each is valid for its own fault and useless for the others.

London's solid walled terraces were built to breathe. Sealing one with cement render or tanking above ground tends to push moisture somewhere else in the building, frequently into the flat next door or the floor above.

Kensington and Chelsea walls, and what they actually need

The borough's terraces are solid London stock brick behind stucco, built in lime. They stay dry when the stucco is sound and breathable, the parapets and valleys are clear, and the front area and vaults drain. Modern basements are engineered structures with membranes, drainage channels and pumps that need servicing. Neither needs an injected damp proof course as a rule.

  • Servicing a basement drainage system: clearing channels, testing pumps, float switches and battery backups
  • Repairing cracked stucco and repainting in a breathable finish, usually as a freeholder or communal matter
  • Clearing and repairing front area drains and pavement vaults
  • Repairing parapet and valley gutters above mansion flats and conversions

Basements: what usually fails

Most basements dug in this borough in recent decades use cavity drainage: a studded membrane on the walls and floor, a perimeter channel and a sump with pumps. The membrane rarely fails. What fails is the maintenance: silted channels, a pump that has not been serviced, a float switch that sticks, or a later alteration that pierced the membrane for a socket or a pipe.

An independent survey identifies which of those it is before anyone proposes stripping out and re tanking, and the report says which parts may still be covered by the original installer's guarantee.

The right order for damp proofing work

  • Establish the cause independently
  • Resolve anything the freeholder or a neighbour is responsible for
  • Fix water entry and ground level faults, then allow the wall to dry
  • Only then specify internal works in the Kensington and Chelsea flat or house, in materials that suit solid walls
  • Check that ventilation and heating allow the wall to stay dry
  • Keep the diagnosis and the guarantee together for the conveyancing file

Warning signs in a damp proofing quote

  • Rising damp diagnosed on an upper floor, or on a party wall with no ground contact
  • No inspection of the lightwell, front area, rear yard or parapet
  • A blanket timber treatment added to a damp quote
  • A guarantee that does not say which walls, or what happens if the cause was elsewhere
  • A price for a Kensington and Chelsea terrace with no reference to its solid walls or its lime plaster
  • Pressure to agree before a buyer's or lender's deadline

When a damp proof course is the right answer

Some Kensington and Chelsea walls do need a damp proof course: a solid wall with no course, or one that has been bridged internally by a solid floor, that remains wet at low level once every external source has been eliminated. Where we find that, the report specifies it plainly, with the height and finish, so you can obtain like for like quotes.

Set the fixed survey fee against the cost of injection, replastering and redecoration in a Kensington and Chelsea property, and against the cost of repeating it when the cause was elsewhere. The comparison usually makes the decision.

The report gives you the cause, the responsibility and a specification. In Kensington and Chelsea that often means a letter to a freeholder or managing agent with the evidence attached, before any work inside the flat is considered.

Damp proofing questions in Kensington and Chelsea

Our basement has started to leak. Does it need re tanking?

Usually not. Drained membrane systems fail at their maintainable parts long before the membrane: channels silt up, pumps fail, float switches stick and battery backups go flat. We survey the whole system and report what has actually failed, which is usually a fraction of the cost of replacement.

The ground floor walls of our stucco house are damp. Is it rising damp?

In these terraces it is far more often a blocked front area drain, a leaking pavement vault, cracked stucco or a raised front path. Each produces a tide mark that looks like rising damp. The survey separates them before any treatment is considered.

Who pays for damp in a mansion flat?

It depends on the lease and the source. Roofs, parapets, external walls and the structure are commonly the freeholder's. Our report identifies the source and the route the water takes, which is what a managing agent needs.

Can you work with a conservation area and listed building constraints?

Yes. The report concentrates on repairs that can be delivered: drainage, rainwater, breathable render and internal finishes, and flags where consent is likely to be needed.

Do you inspect basements for buyers?

Yes, and it is one of the most useful things a buyer can commission here. The report covers the condition of the system, what servicing records to ask for, and any signs of past or current water entry.

Do you recommend waterproofing contractors?

No. We take no referral fees. The report gives a specification any competent contractor can price, and we will read their quotes against it if you ask.

Can you recommend a damp proofing company in Kensington and Chelsea?

We do not refer work or take commission, which keeps the report independent. What we give you instead is a specification precise enough that any competent contractor can price it, and we are happy to read the quotes you receive against it.
