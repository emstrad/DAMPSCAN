# CoolRight

> Air conditioning, heating and ventilation across London and the whole of the
> South East. Installation, repairs and regas, servicing, multi-split systems,
> air to air heat pumps, commercial systems and MVHR. F-Gas registered. Every
> job is quoted after a free visit rather than priced from a rate card.

CoolRight works for homeowners, landlords, managing agents and commercial
occupiers. The recurring theme across the site is that nearly every fault
attended on somebody else's system was built in on the day of installation:
a unit sized from a room's floor area rather than its heat load, condensate
routed with too little fall, a circuit that was never properly evacuated.

## Notes

There is no price list. Every system is quoted after a visit, and the cost
guide publishes the basis instead: what moves the figure, what a quote should
name, and why one quote is cheaper than another.

Cooling does not fix condensation. That is a moisture and ventilation problem
and cooling a surface can make it worse, which the ventilation page and the
cost guide both say directly.
