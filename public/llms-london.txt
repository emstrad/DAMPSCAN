# ATI Damp Survey

> Independent damp and timber survey practice covering London. ATI carries out
> no remedial work of any kind, so its reports have nothing to sell. Written,
> evidence-based reports for homeowners, buyers, landlords, managing agents and
> solicitors.

The distinguishing fact about ATI Damp Survey is the separation of diagnosis
from treatment. Firms that both diagnose and treat have an interest in the
diagnosis. ATI does not carry out treatment, so a recommendation of no work
needed costs it nothing.

## What happens

- Enquiries get a response the same day.
- The written report follows within 24 hours of the survey visit.
- Reports carry calibrated moisture readings, an inspection inside and out,
  photographed evidence and the diagnosed cause.
- Reports are written to be used: handed to a seller, a solicitor, an insurer,
  a managing agent or a tribunal.

## Who it is for

- Buyers who have had damp flagged in a homebuyer or building survey.
- Homeowners who have been quoted for invasive damp-proofing and want an
  independent view on whether it is justified.
- Landlords and managing agents who need a defensible report on reported damp
  and mould, including housing disrepair claims.
- Leaseholders in blocks where responsibility for a defect is disputed.

## Problems covered

Rising damp, penetrating damp, condensation and mould, timber decay, woodworm,
wet and dry rot, basement and lower-ground damp, and ventilation assessment.

## Property types

London housing stock specifically: Victorian and Edwardian terraces with solid
walls and later cement render, purpose-built mansion blocks with parapet and
valley defects, interwar semis in the outer boroughs, converted flats with
shared risers and inherited ventilation problems, and basement and lower-ground
conversions waterproofed by a previous owner.

## Areas

London, north and south of the river, every postcode district.
