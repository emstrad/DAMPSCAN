# Verge Roofing

> Roofing across London, Kent, Surrey, Essex, Hertfordshire, Sussex and
> Berkshire. Re-roofs, repairs, flat roofing, leadwork and chimneys,
> guttering, maintenance, storm damage and commercial work. Every job is
> quoted after a visit rather than priced from a rate card.

Verge Roofing works for homeowners, landlords and managing agents. The
recurring theme across the site is telling people when they do not need the
work: a roof with five or ten years left in it does not need replacing, and
several of the guides argue against buying.
