Independent damp surveys in Newham

Newham was built for the docks and then substantially rebuilt after the war, so its terraces are interrupted by post-war infill in a way few other boroughs match. It also has one of the highest concentrations of private rented housing in the country, and a good share of our work here is producing reports that will be read by somebody other than the person who commissioned them.

Newham housing, and what it does when it gets wet

The surviving Victorian and Edwardian terraces are modest, solid walled and densely occupied. Many are now houses in multiple occupation, and the combination of high occupancy, limited heating and original single glazing produces genuine condensation risk on top of whatever the building itself is doing. Separating the two is the whole job: an overcrowded house with a working extract is a different problem from a house with a cold bridge and no ventilation at all.

Post-war infill and estate housing fills the gaps left by bomb damage. System built and large panel blocks fail at joints and cold bridges, producing mould that runs along slabs and reveals in bands rather than spreading from a point. That geometry is the evidence: mould that traces the structure is telling you about the structure.

Stratford and the Olympic fringe bring recent high density development with airtight envelopes and mechanical ventilation. Where those systems were never commissioned, or have been switched off, moisture accumulates in a building that cannot leak it away, and the result is mould in flats only a few years old. It is a performance failure and it is measurable.

What we are usually called out to in Newham

Which kind of damp is it?

In a London building the same stain can have four or five different causes, and in Newham we regularly find two at once. Water rising from the ground through a wall with no working course. Rain entering through a parapet, a gutter, a cracked render or a window head. Moisture from the air condensing on a cold wall. Water from a pipe, a bath seal or a flat upstairs. Each needs a different repair and, in a shared building, often a different person to pay for it.

Our readings are taken to separate them: at depth as well as at the surface, at heights up the wall, on either side of the damp, and alongside humidity and surface temperatures. The pattern, not a single number, is what the diagnosis rests on.

The inspection, around Stratford and across Newham

  • Every wall showing damp, and the walls either side of it for comparison
  • Rainwater goods and roof junctions from the ground and from windows, and closer where access is safe
  • Basement and lower ground floors for water table, drainage and bridging at the lightwell
  • Condensation risk measured with temperature and humidity readings
  • Visible timber for decay and beetle, with moisture content recorded
  • Anything that would need opening up, identified and agreed with you rather than done unannounced

Newham questions

The property is overcrowded. Does that make the damp the tenant's fault?

It is a factor and it is rarely the whole answer. More occupants means more moisture generated, but a building is expected to deal with the moisture normal occupation produces, and that is what ventilation is for. If the extract moves less air than it should, discharges into a void, or does not exist, the building is not doing its job whatever the occupancy. We measure air flow as well as moisture, so the report separates what the building is failing to do from what occupancy is adding.

We are a landlord. What use is an independent survey to us?

A great deal, particularly before a claim rather than after one. It establishes the actual condition, distinguishes defects you are responsible for from those you are not, and gives you a costed picture of what needs doing. If a claim follows, you already hold a contemporaneous, independent report rather than commissioning one under pressure. We carry out no remedial work, which is what makes the report credible to the other side.

Our Forest Gate terrace has mould in every bedroom. Why?

Often a combination of busy households, limited ventilation and cold external walls. We measure humidity, surface temperatures and extraction so the report can say what will help most.

Is our new build flat in Stratford still under warranty?

Many new homes have a warranty with a period when the developer is responsible for defects. If damp is a defect, an independent report gives the evidence to raise it.

Do you survey HMOs in Newham?

Yes. Houses in multiple occupation must meet housing standards on damp and mould. The report identifies causes and what needs doing.

Will you try to sell me damp proofing in Newham?

No. We carry out no remedial work, so there is nothing to sell after the survey. The report states the cause, the work that would actually fix it and an outline of what that work should reasonably cost, so you can get like for like quotes from contractors of your choice, or put it to a seller, landlord or freeholder.
