Independent damp surveys in Bexleyheath

Bexleyheath, Welling and Barnehurst are suburbs that came with the railway and the motor car, built up mostly in the 1920s and 1930s with a scattering of older cottages and post-war housing. A few landmarks are older, William Morris's Red House and Danson House among them, but the house you are likely to be buying or living in here is an interwar semi or bungalow that has been altered many times.

Bexleyheath housing, and what it does when it gets wet

The typical house is a semi detached pair with a hipped tiled roof, two storey bays at the front, a timber ground floor over a ventilated void and early cavity walls. Many of those cavities have since been filled, and the original metal windows replaced. Filled cavities on a wall facing the weather, or with debris left in them, can carry rain across to the inner leaf, which shows as patches on bedroom walls rather than at floor level.

Almost every house has been extended. Rear kitchens and dining rooms, side garages turned into rooms, conservatories and loft conversions with dormers each add a junction with the original walls and roof. Garage conversions in particular often have a floor lower than the house and walls never meant to be habitable, and they are a frequent source of cold, damp corners.

Barnehurst and parts of Bexleyheath have a good number of bungalows, and there are older Victorian cottages along the main roads and in the pockets that predate the suburbs. Bungalows have a lot of roof and outside wall for their floor area, so cold ceilings and corners are common, and the older cottages bring solid walls and the repairs that have been made to them over a century.

Which kind of damp is it?

In a London building the same stain can have four or five different causes, and in Bexleyheath we regularly find two at once. Water rising from the ground through a wall with no working course. Rain entering through a parapet, a gutter, a cracked render or a window head. Moisture from the air condensing on a cold wall. Water from a pipe, a bath seal or a flat upstairs. Each needs a different repair and, in a shared building, often a different person to pay for it.

Our readings are taken to separate them: at depth as well as at the surface, at heights up the wall, on either side of the damp, and alongside humidity and surface temperatures. The pattern, not a single number, is what the diagnosis rests on.

Bexleyheath questions

Our bedroom wall gets damp patches in wet weather. Could the cavity fill be the cause?

It can be. Insulation fitted into an older cavity can carry rain from the outer wall to the inner one, especially on a wall facing the prevailing weather or where mortar was left in the cavity. Gutters, pointing and window details can do the same. We take readings, look at the outside of the wall and check what the cavity holds where we can, and the report says which it is.

Our converted garage is always cold and musty. Why?

Garages were not built as rooms. The floor is often lower than the house and may have no damp proof membrane, the walls can be a single skin of brick, and the old door opening is a common weak point. We measure the walls and floor and check ventilation, so the report shows what is cold, what is wet and why.

Is mould on bungalow ceilings a sign of a leak?

Sometimes, but more often it is condensation on a cold ceiling where loft insulation is thin, has been disturbed or stops short at the edges. We check the loft and the roof, measure the ceiling temperature and humidity, and tell you which it is.

We are buying a 1930s semi in Welling. What will you look at?

The walls, floors, roof and every addition, with readings throughout. We check the ground levels and airbricks, the condition of the timber floor where we can, the cavity and the junctions with any extension or loft room, so you know what you are buying before you commit.

Will you quote for the repair?

No. We are an independent survey practice and carry out no remedial work. The report sets out what is needed clearly enough for any contractor to price it.

Will you try to sell me damp proofing in Bexleyheath?

No. We carry out no remedial work, so there is nothing to sell after the survey. The report states the cause, the work that would actually fix it and an outline of what that work should reasonably cost, so you can get like for like quotes from contractors of your choice, or put it to a seller, landlord or freeholder.
