Why buyers you send are looked after

Your buyer gets their own page too: the deposit by Apple Pay, their time moved without a phone call, a text when the surveyor is on the way, and the report the moment it is paid for. ATi Damp Survey is independent: we sell no damp treatment, so the report is one your buyer, and the seller, can trust.
