Rising Damp

Rising damp happens when groundwater is drawn up through porous masonry, usually because a damp-proof course has failed or never existed. The giveaways are a tide mark of staining up to about a metre above floor level, salt blooms on the plaster, and skirtings or wallpaper lifting away from the wall.

We diagnose with calibrated moisture readings and a full visual inspection, rule out the other causes first, and only then specify a remedial damp-proof course, salt-resistant render and redecoration. We never recommend chemical injection where it isn't warranted.

Penetrating Damp

Penetrating damp is water coming in sideways through external walls, failed pointing, defective render, leaking gutters and downpipes, or a bridged cavity. You'll normally see isolated patches rather than a tide mark, and it gets noticeably worse after sustained rain.

Our surveyors find the entry point first, frequently outside the room you're seeing the damage in, and fix the underlying defect before touching the internal finishes. The repair lasts because the cause has been dealt with, not just the symptom.

Condensation

Condensation is the most common form of damp in modern, well-sealed homes: warm moist air meeting cold surfaces and leaving water behind. Misted windows, damp patches in cold corners, a musty smell. Left alone it almost always turns into mould.

We measure relative humidity, surface temperatures and air movement to confirm it, then recommend a targeted ventilation strategy, from extract upgrades to a Positive Input Ventilation unit, with practical guidance for day-to-day use of the property.

Mould Removal

Mould is the visible end-stage of an environmental damp problem, most often condensation. Wiping it away without fixing the conditions behind it just lets it come back, usually worse and usually further across the wall.

We treat affected surfaces with a professional fungicidal wash, prepare and repaint with anti-mould coatings where appropriate, and pair the works with a ventilation strategy so it doesn't return. Where the real cause is structural damp, we deal with that first.

Woodworm / Timber

Woodworm is the larvae of several wood-boring beetles tunnelling through structural and decorative timbers. Small round exit holes, fine bore dust beneath joists and weakened timber are the classic signs, and the damage is usually further along than it looks.

We identify the species, establish whether the infestation is active or historic, and treat only what needs treating with a low-odour residual insecticide. Where structural timbers are compromised we work with trusted joiners on the repairs.

Wet & Dry Rot

Wet rot and dry rot are both fungal decay in damp timber, but they behave very differently. Dry rot spreads through masonry to find new timber and needs decisive treatment; wet rot stays local to the moisture source. Getting the identification right changes the entire scope of work.

We confirm which you have, trace and eliminate the moisture feeding it, cut out and replace affected timber, and treat the surrounding structure. We won't specify a full dry-rot programme for something that is simply wet rot around a leaking pipe.

Waterproofing & Tanking

Below-ground spaces, basements, cellars, retrofitted living space, need waterproofing designed as a system, not patched with a coating. We design to BS 8102, specifying Type A (barrier), Type B (structurally integral) or Type C (drained cavity) as the build-up requires.

That can mean cementitious tanking slurry, membrane systems, cavity drainage with sump and pump, or a combination, plus ventilation to control the resulting humidity. Every project is documented so future works to the room don't compromise the waterproofing.

Ventilation

Most modern homes are over-sealed and under-ventilated, which is exactly why condensation and mould are so common. The fix isn't obvious either, opening windows isn't a strategy that survives a British winter.

We design ventilation to match the property, from humidity-sensing extract fans to whole-house Positive Input Ventilation. Every installation is sized, balanced and commissioned properly so it does its job quietly in the background.

Book a Survey

Rendering & Plastering

Blown plaster and damaged render are usually a symptom of a damp problem rather than the problem itself. Replacing the finish without addressing the cause is a short-lived fix that ends up being paid for twice.

We renew in lime, sand-and-cement or modern proprietary systems, matched to the property's age and construction, and only once any damp beneath has been resolved. The result is a finish that stays sound long term.

Straight answers

The questions everyone asks us.

Will this actually fix the problem?

That depends entirely on getting the diagnosis right, which is why we survey before we quote. When the true cause is identified and treated properly, the problem doesn't come back. Most repeat damp problems we're called to are second visits to work that treated a symptom.

What's the guarantee?

All remedial work we carry out is covered by an insured workmanship guarantee, with documentation you can hand on to a buyer or your insurer. The exact term depends on the treatment, it's stated clearly on your quote before you commit to anything.

What happens after I submit the form?

A surveyor reviews your details and contacts you the same day to arrange a visit. Within 24 hours of that visit you get a written report with photographs, the diagnosed cause and a fixed-price quote. Then you decide, there's no obligation and nobody will chase you.

How fast can you come out?

We respond to every enquiry the same day and arrange the visit around you, then your written report follows within 24 hours of that visit. We cover Kent, Surrey, Essex, Sussex, Hertfordshire and Berkshire, and can usually work around evenings and weekends.

Is the work messy?

Some treatments involve removing plaster or lifting floor coverings, so there is dust, but we sheet up, protect finishes and clean down at the end of each day. Your report tells you exactly what will be disturbed before any work starts.

Can we stay in the house while work is carried out?

In the overwhelming majority of cases, yes. We work room by room so you keep the rest of the property. If a job would genuinely make a room unusable for a period, we'll tell you upfront rather than on the day.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents?

Yes, a large share of our work is certified inspections for landlords and managing agents dealing with reported damp and mould, including portfolio surveys across multiple properties. See the landlord section above.

Which areas do you cover?

Kent and the wider South East, including Surrey, Essex, East and West Sussex, Hertfordshire, Berkshire and Buckinghamshire. Properties inside London are surveyed by our sister practice, ATi Damp Survey, at atidampsurvey.co.uk. Enter your postcode in the booking form and we'll confirm cover straight away.

What if I just want a second opinion?

That's one of the most useful things we do. If you've been quoted for invasive damp-proofing and it doesn't feel right, we'll survey independently and tell you honestly whether that work is justified.
