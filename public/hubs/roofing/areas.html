Where we work, and what the roofs are like there

Our own team covers every London borough and the whole of the South East. The roofs are not the same everywhere: Victorian slate behind a parapet in Hackney, interwar concrete tile in Bexley, Kent peg on a village cottage and Regency slate on the Brighton seafront all fail in different ways.

Each page below is about one region: what was built there, what the roofs are covered in, what tends to go wrong with them, and the towns we work in. If your town is not listed, send your postcode anyway.

The quote visit is free wherever you are, and the price comes back fixed and in writing.

Every area, in detail

  • Essex and east London Re-roofs, extension flat roofs, leadwork and storm repairs in Romford, Brentwood, Basildon, Chelmsford, Ilford and Southend. Free quotes, fixed in writing.
  • Hertfordshire and north west London Re-roofs, bay roofs, valleys and dormer flat roofs on 1930s semis in Watford, St Albans, Hemel Hempstead, Borehamwood and Hatfield. Free fixed quotes.
  • Kent and south east London Re-roofs, repairs, flat roofs, chimneys and leadwork in Bromley, Bexley, Dartford, Sevenoaks, Maidstone and Medway. Free quotes, fixed in writing.
  • North and east London Slate re-roofs, butterfly roofs, parapet gutters and extension flat roofs in Islington, Hackney, Walthamstow, Tottenham and Leyton. Free fixed quotes.
  • Surrey and south west London Clay tile and slate re-roofs, tile hanging, dormers and flat roofs in Croydon, Kingston, Sutton, Epsom, Guildford, Woking and Reigate. Free fixed quotes.
  • Sussex and Berkshire Re-roofs, repairs, slate and tile, flat roofs and chimneys in Crawley, Horsham, Brighton, Slough, Windsor and Reading. Free quotes, fixed in writing.
