What we inspect, and what the report will say about it

Most people arrive here already holding a quote. The question is rarely whether the wall is wet, which is usually obvious, but whether the work being proposed matches the reason it is wet.

Each page below sets out one defect: what establishes it as that defect rather than another, what evidence a report should carry to support the conclusion, and what a proportionate repair looks like.

We undertake no remedial work of any kind. That is the whole basis of the practice, and the reason these pages describe repairs we will never be paid to carry out.

Every service, in detail

  • Basement Damp Independent basement and lower ground damp surveys across London. Second opinions on tanking and re-waterproofing quotes from a practice that installs nothing.
  • Condensation and Mould Independent condensation and mould reports across London, distinguishing building defects from occupation. Written for landlords, leaseholders and disrepair.
  • Independent Damp Surveys Independent damp surveys across London for buyers, owners, landlords, agents and solicitors. We carry out no remedial work, so the report has nothing to sell.
  • Penetrating Damp Independent surveys establishing where water enters a London building and whose repair it is. Written reports for owners, leaseholders and solicitors.
  • Rising Damp An independent view on whether a wall has rising damp, across London. We carry out no remedial work, so a report saying none is needed costs us nothing.
  • Timber Decay and Woodworm Independent timber and woodworm reports across London. We carry out no treatment, so a finding that the infestation is historic costs us nothing.
  • Ventilation Independent ventilation assessments across London, with measured flow rates. Reports for landlords, leaseholders, disrepair claims and new build defects.
  • Wet and Dry Rot Independent wet and dry rot reports across London. Extent established before scope is set, by a practice that carries out no remedial work.
